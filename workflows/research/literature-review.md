# Literature Review Workflow

## Goal

Use Claude to organize a literature review while preserving traceability to source material.

## Stages

### 1. Define the question

Write a narrow research question and the population/system/domain being studied.

### 2. Collect sources

Provide papers, abstracts, notes, or references. Keep source identifiers attached to each document.

### 3. Extract evidence

Ask Claude to extract the research question, method, findings, limitations, and evidence for each source.

### 4. Compare

Group sources by theme and explicitly identify agreement, disagreement, and uncertainty.

### 5. Find gaps

Ask for unanswered questions, methodological weaknesses, and opportunities for further study.

### 6. Draft

Generate a structured review from the evidence table.

### 7. Verify

A human should verify citations, quotations, numerical results, and interpretations against the original papers.

## Failure cases

- Missing source text: do not ask Claude to invent the missing details.
- Conflicting studies: preserve the disagreement and inspect methods.
- Weak evidence: label the conclusion as uncertain.
