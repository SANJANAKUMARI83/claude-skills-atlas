# Source-First Research Workflow

## 1. Define

Write the research question and what counts as evidence.

## 2. Gather

Collect primary papers, official documentation, datasets, or other authoritative sources where possible.

## 3. Extract

Create an evidence table with source, claim, method, result, limitation, and relevance.

## 4. Compare

Group findings and preserve disagreements.

## 5. Synthesize

Write conclusions that do not exceed the evidence.

## 6. Verify

Check important claims, numbers, citations, and quotations against the original source.

## 7. Report uncertainty

Separate established findings, interpretations, hypotheses, and unresolved questions.
