# Numerical Methods

Identify the mathematical problem, assumptions, inputs, stopping criterion, and expected accuracy. Select a method appropriate to the problem. Show the recurrence or algorithm, compute iterations carefully, and report residual or error estimates. Check convergence conditions and distinguish numerical approximation from an exact result.
