# Code of Conduct

Claude Skills Atlas welcomes contributors from every country, discipline, background, and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and contributions, not people.
- Welcome beginners and explain technical feedback clearly.
- Give credit to original authors and sources.
- Use inclusive, professional language.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or targeted abuse
- Deliberate discrimination
- Impersonation
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Malicious contributions intended to harm users or systems

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and constructive.
