# Research Paper Reading Prompt

```text
Read the supplied paper as a critical research assistant.

Paper:
{{paper}}

My focus:
{{question}}

Return:
1. Research problem
2. Hypothesis / objective
3. Methods
4. Data or experimental setup
5. Main results
6. Evidence supporting each major conclusion
7. Limitations
8. Assumptions
9. What is genuinely novel
10. Two questions I should investigate next

For every important statement, distinguish:
- explicitly reported by the paper
- reasonable interpretation
- speculation / proposed follow-up

Do not invent details that are missing from the supplied paper.
```
