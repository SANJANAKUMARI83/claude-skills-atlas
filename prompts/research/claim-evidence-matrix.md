# Claim-Evidence Matrix

Use this prompt to turn research notes into an auditable evidence table.

~~~text
Extract the important factual claims from the material I provide.

For each claim, record:
- exact claim
- source
- source type
- publication date
- evidence supporting it
- scope or population
- limitations
- contradictory evidence, if found
- verification status

Do not strengthen a claim beyond what the source supports.
Separate established findings, interpretations, and unresolved questions.
Flag claims that require a primary source.
~~~
