# Turn a Successful Session Into a Skill

Use this prompt after completing a repeatable task successfully.

~~~text
Analyze the successful workflow in this session.

Identify:
- the recurring objective
- the sequence of actions that mattered
- decisions that required judgment
- failure modes and recovery steps
- inputs that vary between projects
- checks that verify success

Then propose an original reusable SKILL.md.

Keep project-specific names, credentials, paths, and one-off details out of the skill.
Do not create the skill yet. First show the proposed frontmatter, workflow, examples, and verification plan.
~~~
