# Active Learning Prompt

```text
Teach me {{topic}} at {{level}}.

First, determine the smallest set of prerequisites I need.

Then use this loop:
1. Explain one concept briefly.
2. Give one worked example.
3. Ask me a short question.
4. Wait for my answer.
5. Correct only what is wrong or incomplete.
6. Increase difficulty gradually.

At the end of each section, give me 3 practice questions.
Do not give their solutions unless I ask.

Prioritize accuracy and understanding over excessive length.
```
